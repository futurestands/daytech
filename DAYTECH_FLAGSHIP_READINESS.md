# DayTech flagship readiness assessment

**Recommendation** Start with a narrowly scoped HotelOS pilot evaluation. It is the only candidate with a separately identified application codebase. Keep it separate from the corporate website. This is a development priority, with commercial launch gated on rights, security and demand.

| Area | HotelOS | Standalone Inventory & POS |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Source | Reviewed public `futurestands/HOTELMANAGEMENT` at `bc7f087`. React, Vite and Supabase/PostgreSQL, including 18 SQL migrations and a separate application entry. | No standalone implementation was identified in the inspected workspace or public repository names. The corporate catalogue documents the concept. HotelOS has hospitality ordering and stock-related code, which does not establish a standalone retail POS. |
| Existing evidence | Source shows tenant onboarding, rooms, guests, reservations, folios/payments, inventory, menu, staff, membership and audit panels. The migrations define reservation overlap protection and tenant/RLS policy work. End-to-end behaviour was not run. | Catalogue and illustrative data describe barcode checkout, transfers, branches and shift reconciliation. Operational features, hardware support and backend were not verified. |
| Offline and mobile | CSS breakpoints at 900 px and 680 px. No offline queue, service worker or IndexedDB-backed sync was identified. Mobile interaction and reconnect behaviour need device testing. | Offline replay, conflict resolution, receipt printer/barcode support and cashier-device performance remain unknown without source. |
| Testing and security | Four configured integration cases cover tenant isolation, roles, membership boundaries and room constraints. The suite requires Supabase test configuration and a privileged setup identity. It was not run against live or existing credentials. Verify all migrations together, RLS across financial and guest records, reservation/folio consistency under concurrency, audit integrity, backups and restores. | Verify financial totals, cashier role limits, shift reconciliation, inventory consistency, hardware and idempotent retries if source is located. |
| Licensing and infrastructure | Separate Supabase Auth and PostgreSQL environment with explicit backup and recovery requirements. No repository-level licence was found in the inspected tree. Confirm contributor and employer separation, product code rights, dependency notices and data handling before using it commercially. | Confirm repository ownership, dependencies and provider/hardware licence terms once the implementation is found. |
| Uganda fit and recurring model | Interview independent properties to establish pain around reservations, front-desk handovers and room folios. Validate a per-property monthly subscription and separate onboarding charge with a pilot buyer. | Validate one retail segment and per-outlet pricing with real buyers. Offline resilience, receipts, fiscal compliance and hardware support are material effort drivers. |

**Pilot scope.** Take one property, staff access, rooms, reservations and auditable folio review through a synthetic acceptance exercise. Review periodic reporting, client export, recovery and a practical operating procedure for outages. Hold broader HR, card payments, retail POS and offline synchronisation outside that first scope.

**Planning estimate.** Allow one engineering week for source/licence review, a clean synthetic setup and gap confirmation. If the baseline holds, budget a further three to five engineering weeks to reach a narrow pilot-ready candidate. This is a planning range, with offline sync, payment integration and unresolved ownership outside it. Standalone POS effort cannot be reliably estimated until its source and hardware requirements are available. Recurring revenue should be modelled as validated monthly price per property multiplied by committed paying properties. No subscription price, paying-customer count or sales forecast was verified.

Before a real deployment, confirm applicable fiscal receipting with URA and confirm DayTech's and each customer's data-handling obligations with the PDPO or appropriate advisers. URA identifies VAT-registered businesses and certain designated sectors as requiring EFRIS. Review requirements before positioning product receipts as fiscal documents.
